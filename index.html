WordPocket Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

In short

WordPocket keeps your words on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and the app has no internet permission, so it can't send your words to us. We never receive them.

What the app stores on your device

  • Words you add, with their translations, examples, notes, part of speech and dictionary names.
  • Your review schedule and how many answers you gave each day, which power reviews, your daily goal, your streak and your stats.
  • Your settings: the languages you chose, your daily goal, your reminder time and your theme.

This information stays in the app's private storage. We don't collect it, sell it or share it.

Device backups

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include WordPocket's data in your device backup, for example in your Google account. That backup is controlled by you and handled by your device and backup provider, not by us. You can manage it in your device settings.

Pronunciation

When you tap a speaker icon, the app passes that word to the text-to-speech engine on your device. That engine is provided separately by your device maker or another provider, and its own privacy terms apply.

Importing and exporting

The app only reads or creates a CSV file when you choose one in Android's file picker. Exported files are not encrypted, and contain your words and review progress. Where you save or share them is up to you.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked for only if you turn on the daily review reminder, and used only for that reminder. You can refuse and use the rest of the app normally.
  • Background scheduling: the system library that schedules reminders also declares permissions for running after a restart, keeping the device awake briefly, background work and checking network state. WordPocket doesn't use them to send data anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete single words in the app, or everything from Settings > Delete all words. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete. Copies in your device backups or exported files are yours to remove.

Children

WordPocket doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
